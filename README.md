# 🛒 Store Data Analysis Dashboard (Excel Project)

## 🔹 Project Overview
This Excel project analyzes **2022 sales data** for a retail store to uncover trends, customer behavior, and top-performing products and regions.  
It delivers actionable insights through an **interactive dashboard**, helping businesses make data-driven decisions for 2023.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
The dashboard answers critical business questions:  
- How do **Sales and Orders** vary month-to-month?  
- Which **customer demographics** drive the most revenue?  
- What are the **top 10 states** contributing to sales?  
- Which **sales channels** perform best?  
- Which **product categories** are most profitable?

---

---

## ⚙️ Project Methodology

### 1️⃣ Data Cleaning
- Removed duplicates and handled missing values  
- Standardized column names and formats (Date, Category, Channel, etc.)  
- Verified data consistency for accuracy  

### 2️⃣ Data Analysis
- Created **Pivot Tables** to summarize sales by Month, Gender, Age, State, and Channel  
- Calculated **Total Sales**, **Order Count**, and **Profit %**  
- Compared performance across multiple dimensions  

### 3️⃣ Dashboard Creation
- Developed a **clean, interactive Excel dashboard**  
- Added **Slicers** for filtering by Gender, Age Group, State, Month, and Channel  
- Used **Pivot Charts** to visualize trends and insights effectively

## 🔍 Key Insights
| Area | Insight |
|------|---------|
| 👩 Gender | Women account for ~65% of total purchases |
| 🗓️ Month | March recorded highest sales & orders |
| 🏙️ States | Maharashtra, Karnataka & Uttar Pradesh lead |
| 👨‍🦳 Age Group | Adults (30–49 yrs) contribute ~50% of sales |
| 🛒 Sales Channel | Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra drive majority revenue |
| 📦 Product | Clothing & Footwear are top-selling categories |

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Focus marketing on **female customers aged 30–49**  
- Increase promotions in **Maharashtra, Karnataka & Uttar Pradesh**  
- Provide special offers on **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**  
- Run **seasonal campaigns** during peak months
